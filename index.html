GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations are part of a premium service. Here is what to know before you book.

How long does carpet take to dry?+

Most carpets dry in about 4 to 6 hours. Drying time can change with fabric, ventilation, soil level, and the treatment needed.

Are the products safe for kids and pets?+

The service uses non-toxic, biodegradable solutions. Once the treated surface is dry, everyone can use the space as usual.

Is the estimate the final price?+

The site provides a starting estimate. The final scope and price are reviewed with you before work begins.

What should I tell you about?+

Pet accidents, drink spills, smoke, heavy traffic areas, strong odors, delicate fibers, and old stains help the team prepare the right treatment.
